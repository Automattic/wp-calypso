## Editorial mix

The agreed list combines six faith traditions with independent news and scholarly discussion. Christianity and Judaism each have two sources; Islam, Buddhism, Hinduism and the Bahá’í faith each have one. Four further sources cover religion through news or scholarship. This is a varied introduction, not a complete representation of all faiths or all views within any tradition.

Descriptions identify faith-based perspectives. News and academic discussion sit alongside religious community reporting and practice; no source is selected solely to promote conversion or attack another faith. Kveller’s public RSS URL is kept separate from the homepage alias used to obtain its metadata ID.
