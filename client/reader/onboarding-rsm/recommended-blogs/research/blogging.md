## Editorial mix

This list covers four practical parts of blogging: writing and content craft, WordPress publishing, discovery through search, and building an audience. It combines specialist publications, Seth Godin’s individual daily essays, SEO vendors and marketing agencies. The latter have commercial interests; they are selected for relevant publishing guidance, not presented as independent assessments of their own products.

The specialist candidate pool was checked first. ProBlogger’s newest feed item was in 2024, Smart Blogger returned HTTP 520, BloggingPro returned HTTP 403, and Content Marketing Institute’s response did not parse as XML. These results describe this public fetch only. The final mix favors active practical publishing resources over inactive how-to blogs; it does not claim to represent every style of personal blogging.
