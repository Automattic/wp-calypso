## Editorial mix and rating limits

The agreed mix is four Center or nonpartisan sources, four left-leaning sources, and four right-leaning sources. This is a US-focused editorial grouping, not a claim of mathematical equivalence or guaranteed neutrality. The side descriptions identify their perspective.

AllSides rates the Center selections Center. The left allocation contains three Left and one Lean Left rating; the right allocation contains two Lean Right ratings, National Review’s separate News Lean Right / Opinion Right ratings, and The American Conservative without an established AllSides rating. Ad Fontes scores and category labels are recorded individually where available, without inventing missing scores or averaging unlike scopes. Several ratings concern the whole publisher rather than the precise category feed. AllSides labels Slow Boring’s rating low/initial confidence.

Ad Fontes’ scores between 24 and 36 can reflect opinion-heavy content or variable reliability; they do not establish that every article is reliable. National Review’s 31.38 is explicitly in its mixed-reliability/opinion category. No selected publisher has a measured Ad Fontes score below 24. Opinion sources are named as such, alongside reporting and nonpartisan research.

SCOTUSblog remains a reserve because its publisher confirms acquisition by The Dispatch in April 2025; including both would duplicate an owner across the Center and conservative allocations. Its low/initial-confidence AllSides profile cites an About page retrieved before that acquisition. Newsweek uses its working public `/rss.xml` feed; the metadata lookup uses the historical `/rss` alias only.
